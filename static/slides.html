Problem

Vendor approval is a coordination problem, not just a reasoning problem.

Work is fragmentedProcurement, finance, security, legal, and leadership review the same request in separate systems.
Context is lostCritical evidence, objections, and signoff conditions get buried across messages and spreadsheets.
Risk is lateSecurity and legal concerns often appear after teams already want the vendor approved.

Solution

Band becomes the shared room where agents coordinate the approval.

Agents do not simply run in parallel. They route work with @mentions, hand off artifacts, create shared context, challenge the packet, and preserve the audit trail.

CoordinatorOpens the case and recruits specialists.
SpecialistsVendor, finance, security, and legal produce structured handoffs.
Evidence ClerkCreates one shared context packet.
Red Team AuditorChallenges or blocks unsafe assumptions.
Approval ManagerProduces a decision, human gate, report, and audit seal.

Why It Fits The Challenge

Visible collaboration is the product surface.

Band as coordination layer@Mention routing, shared room messages, handoffs, task state, and participants are all visible.
Cross-framework storyEach agent declares a framework profile, with Band acting as the common collaboration layer.
Business workflowProcurement approval maps directly to internal enterprise workflows and regulated decision-making.

Differentiators

More than a generic vendor-risk chatbot.

  • Evidence Clerk creates a shared context packet before decision-making.
  • Red Team Auditor can force escalation when the packet is not cleared.
  • Human Procurement Lead gate records signoffs and blocked approval states.
  • SHA-256 audit seal anchors the case, artifacts, context, challenge, decision, gate, and timeline.
  • Downloadable report gives judges a real enterprise artifact.
