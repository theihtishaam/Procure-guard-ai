The Problem

Why vendor approval breaks down

Approving a vendor is a coordination problem, not just a reasoning problem.

1

Work is fragmented

Procurement, finance, security, legal, and leadership review the same request in separate tools and email threads.

2

Context is lost

Evidence, objections, and signoff conditions get buried across messages and spreadsheets. Nothing is the source of truth.

3

Risk arrives late

Security and legal concerns surface after the team already wants the vendor approved, so guardrails get skipped.

A procurement decision court

One shared Band room where specialist agents coordinate the whole approval.

Instead of a single chatbot answering a prompt, ProcureGuard opens a review room. Agents route work with @mentions, hand off structured findings with cited evidence, build one shared context packet, run an adversarial review, then hand a sealed decision to a human approver.

How It Works

End-to-end workflow

Six stages, from intake to a sealed human decision.

STEP 1

Intake

Coordinator opens the case and recruits the review roles.

STEP 2

Specialists

Vendor, finance, security & legal review in parallel.

STEP 3

Evidence

Clerk consolidates findings into one shared packet.

STEP 4

Red Team

Auditor challenges gaps and unsafe assumptions.

STEP 5

Decision

Approval Manager writes the recommendation.

STEP 6

Human Gate

Procurement Lead signs off; case is sealed.

Each stage produces an artifact that the next stage builds on — so the output is a decision packet, not a wall of text.
